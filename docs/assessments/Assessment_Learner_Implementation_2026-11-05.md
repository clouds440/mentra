### Assessment quality: actual small before/after review

Internal factual/key/solvability audit: **14/15 before versus 15/15 after** (five physics, five Python, five Arabic items per version). All physics and Python keys were correct in both samples. Before Arabic item 2 asks which letter has *two dots below* and keys **ب**, falsely explaining that ba has two dots. None of its choices is valid. The after sample has no such defect. Admission v2 now deterministically rejects this exact finite Arabic-letter dot-count/key contradiction; regression tests also verify correct cases and abstention on unrelated geometry. This is a real defect and a tested fix, not a claim that a 15-item sample validates all Arabic pedagogy.

Grading agreement on the same simple known-rubric corpus is **45/45 questions before and 45/45 after**, including correct, partial and wrong written answers. Normalized score error was zero in both samples. Grading accuracy is preserved, not shown to improve generally. Choice grading now skips model admission and a redundant deterministic checkpoint: it completes even when model capacity is unavailable, while durable answers, publication fences and the background evidence outbox remain intact. Written batching, complete rubrics, partial-credit checks and resumable checkpoints remain protected.

### Learner usefulness, quality and roadmaps

Before the audit, 88 recorded evidence rows were all formal quizzes and 19 chat capture jobs were ignored. Completed-turn source capture now retains exact spans, source/extraction revisions and pending low-confidence observations; it never invents mastery from mentions, copied work or self-report. Naming an unknown skill during chat is a read-only lookup, with no foreground organizer or canonical-skill creation. Explicit reviewed bindings, bounded prompt selection, guarded cleanup and a current-state scalar index replace request-time lifetime scans and silent concept loss.

Roadmap packets now retain goal importance/relevance, misconceptions, independent-item support and target-difficulty support. At least one relevant source observation gets space before scored skills can fill the packet. The model is instructed to distinguish observed knowledge from unknown ability, diagnose unsupported prerequisites and give observable milestones. Exact repeated item content cannot manufacture fresh independent mastery, even when generated under new assessment/question IDs; raw answers and evidence remain retained.

Three same-goal synthetic roadmap pairs were reviewed internally using five explicit criteria (evidence honesty, appropriate sequencing, targeting the supplied struggle, matching observable checkpoints, goal/timeframe). **No evidence: 2/3 → 3/3; foundation misconception: 2/5 → 4/5; demonstrated lists plus unverified joins: 2/5 → 5/5.** The after model labels missing evidence provisional, focuses on concatenation confusion, and starts joins with a duplicate-row diagnostic rather than asserting the student knows or does not know it. One after checkpoint asks for an awkward `[4,2]` loop and loses a point. These are non-blinded assistant rubric scores from three synthetic pairs, not independent review or proof of learning benefit. Full outputs and reasons are in the comparison artifact. G15/G16 remain held.
